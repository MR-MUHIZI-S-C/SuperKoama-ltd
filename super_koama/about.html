Company Overview

SUPER KOAMA COMPANY LIMITED is a duly registered private limited liability company incorporated in The United Republic of Tanzania under the Business Registrations and Licensing Agency (BRELA).

The company operates as a supplier and wholesaler of construction and industrial raw materials, serving manufacturers, contractors and infrastructure developers across Tanzania.

The company was established with the objective of supporting the growing construction and material sectors in Tanzania by ensuring the reliable supply of high-quality raw materials essential for production and infrastructure development.

SUPER KOAMA COMPANY LIMITED is committed to professionalism, reliability, and compliance with all applicable laws and regulations governing business operations in Tanzania.
