Construction Materials

Super Koama Company Limited supplies construction materials to contractors, builders and developers across Tanzania. We focus on dependable supply and consistent quality so your site is never held up waiting for materials.

We source from trusted manufacturers and suppliers and aim to offer competitive prices without compromising on quality. Delivery arrangements are agreed at quotation to suit your site schedule.

This service includes:

  • Cement (saruji) in various types and brands
  • Steel reinforcement bars and mesh (nondo)
  • Sand and aggregates (mchanga, kokoto)
  • Concrete blocks and bricks (matofali)
  • Roofing sheets and accessories (mabati)
  • Timber and lumber (mbao)
  • Plumbing and electrical supplies
  • Interior and finishing materials
