Welcome to Super Koama

Super Koama Company Limited is a private limited liability company incorporated in the United Republic of Tanzania under the Business Registrations and Licensing Agency (BRELA). We operate as a supplier and wholesaler of construction and industrial raw materials, serving manufacturers, contractors and infrastructure developers across Tanzania.

We are committed to professionalism, reliability and full compliance with the laws and regulations that govern business in Tanzania.
